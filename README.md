# 数学十字谜 (Math Crosswords)

A crossword-style arithmetic puzzle game. Fill the grid with numbers so every horizontal and vertical equation holds.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play — no build step, no server needed.

## How to play

- Tap a blank cell, then tap a number tile from the bank below — or drag a tile into a cell.
- Every equation must hold: e.g. 3 + 5 = 8, 12 + 23 = 35.
- Multiplication and addition are commutative (5 × 7 and 7 × 5 both count); division swaps divisor and quotient (18 ÷ 9 = 2 and 18 ÷ 2 = 9 both count).
- A wrong number stays in its cell, marked red — tap the cell to take it back, or drag a tile onto it to replace it. An equation is only flagged wrong once it is filled and its numbers don't match.
- 3 mistakes ends the round.

## Features

- Four difficulties: easy, medium, hard, expert (up to 11×11, numbers up to 150)
- Sort the number bank, undo one step at a time, reset the round, pause, hints
- Timer, a sound toggle for the casino-style placement sound and win jingle
- A fresh random puzzle every time you open it
